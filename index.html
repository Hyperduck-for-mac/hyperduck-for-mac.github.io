How It Works

1. Enable iCloud
Sign in with the same Apple ID on your iOS device and Mac.

2. Share a Link
On iPhone or iPad, tap Share and select Hyperduck from the sheet.

3. Instant Delivery
The link queues if offline and opens automatically in your Mac's default browser when ready.

4. Customize (Optional)
Set up Mac Shortcuts for advanced actions triggered by incoming links.
